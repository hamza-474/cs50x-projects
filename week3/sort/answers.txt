sort1 uses: bubble sort

How do you know?: Sorted 50000 numbers in about 20 ms, but reversed 50000 took about 5.5 seconds and random 50000 about 6 seconds, so it is fast only on an already sorted list and quadratic otherwise.

sort2 uses: merge sort

How do you know?: Sorted, reversed, and random 50000 numbers all took only about 20 to 40 ms, far faster than the other two sorts on large inputs, as expected for an O(n log n) sort.

sort3 uses: selection sort

How do you know?: Sorted, reversed, and random 50000 numbers all took about 2.2 seconds, so its time does not depend on the input order, as expected for selection sort, which always scans the unsorted part.
